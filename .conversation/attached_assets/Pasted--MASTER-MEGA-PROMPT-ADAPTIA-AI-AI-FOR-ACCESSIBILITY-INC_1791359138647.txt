# 2. CORE PROBLEM

Digital information is increasingly available, but availability does not guarantee accessibility, understandability, or language inclusivity.

Information is commonly published in standardized formats even though users differ in:

* How they see information
* How they hear information
* How they read information
* How they understand information
* Which language they understand best
* How they navigate information
* How they interact with technology
* How much complexity they can comfortably process

Examples:

* A visually oriented document may be difficult to access without meaningful descriptions.
* Audio/video may be inaccessible without transcripts or captions.
* Complex language may create cognitive barriers.
* English-only information may exclude users who understand another language better.
* Poorly structured documents may be difficult for assistive technologies.
* Long information may be difficult to navigate.
* A translated document may lose important dates, names, numbers, or instructions.
* Different scripts require different rendering and layout behavior.

Existing tools frequently solve these challenges independently.

ADAPTIA AI addresses the broader problem:

> **How can one AI system understand information, understand the user's selected accessibility and language preferences, dynamically decide what adaptations are necessary, produce accessible multilingual representations, and verify that the original meaning has been preserved?**
